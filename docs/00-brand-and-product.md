# ILMOFAHM — Brand and Product Identity

## Canonical identity

- **Institutional label:** `INSTITUTIONAL TECHNOLOGY`
- **Product name / wordmark:** `ILMOFAHM`
- **Tagline:** `One system. Every institution.`
- **Product descriptor:** `Centralized school and academy management, designed for complete institutional visibility.`
- **Product category:** Multi-tenant school and academy management SaaS.
- **Initial market:** Pakistan; architecture should not hard-code country-specific behavior.

## Exact copy rules

- Always write the product name exactly as `ILMOFAHM` in user-facing product copy.
- Use `INSTITUTIONAL TECHNOLOGY` as the small uppercase brand label where a brand lockup is shown.
- Use the tagline with the period exactly as shown: `One system. Every institution.`
- Use the product descriptor verbatim on the initial public landing/placeholder page until the product owner approves alternative marketing copy.
- Do not use the retired name or derive new branding from it. Requirement IDs start with `ILM-`.
- Repository name recommendation: `ilmofahm` (confirm availability before creating it).

## Initial visual direction

Use a restrained, modern enterprise visual style appropriate for school owners and administrators. Prioritize readability, visible hierarchy, accessible contrast, and calm operational confidence. A provisional deep-ink/navy and teal palette may be used as design tokens, but keep it easy to change after a design review. Do not fabricate partner logos, certifications, customer logos, statistics, or feature screenshots.

Use the text wordmark initially; do not invent a complex symbol or claim a trademarked logo exists. Keep brand text as real HTML text for accessibility and responsiveness. Maintain a clear separation between the marketing/entry page and authenticated institutional workspaces.

## Product language

- Say **institution** for the tenant/customer account where the wording must cover both schools and academies.
- Say **school or academy** when describing institution types explicitly.
- Distinguish ILMOFAHM subscription billing from any institution's fee collections from students.
- Avoid wording that implies modules have launched before they are implemented and tested.
